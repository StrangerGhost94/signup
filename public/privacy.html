Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email, phone number, and password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text). Pros additionally provide a trade category, service area, bio, and optionally a profile photo. If you use Google sign-in, we receive your name and email from Google.

2. Location data

If you choose to share your device's location, we use it to sort search results by distance or to display your service area to clients. Location sharing is optional and only happens after you explicitly allow it through your browser.

3. How we use your data

We use your information to operate the matching, booking, messaging, and review features of HandyLink, to send you account-related notifications, and to respond to support requests.

4. Messages

Messages sent through HandyLink are stored so both participants in a booking can see the conversation history. We don't read messages except as needed to investigate abuse reports.

5. Sharing

We don't sell your personal data. Your name, category, location, rating, and reviews are visible to other users as part of the normal operation of the marketplace. We don't share your data with third parties for advertising.

6. Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time from your Profile page. This permanently removes your account and associated data, including bookings, messages, and reviews tied to your account.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. We use HTTPS to encrypt data in transit and rate-limit login attempts to reduce abuse.

8. Your choices

You can update your name and phone number at any time from your Profile page, and stop sharing your location at any time through your browser or device settings.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Support page in your account.
